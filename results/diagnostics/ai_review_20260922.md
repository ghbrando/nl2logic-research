matter expert is available. This is **AI-attributed exploratory annotation**,
not human or SME validation. Every exported row has `reviewed_by` set to
`Codex (AI; not SME)` and `reviewer_type` set to `ai`. The original draft
worksheets and candidates remain unchanged. Review duration is blank because
it was not measured.

that every selected passage matches the numbered paragraph on its recorded PDF
page after normalizing whitespace and punctuation. The FM 2-0 (2023) PDF

`10a08e6ff0779c7d6c37b4d05f3ab813fcbd86f78d3eefc5348d19e42511827d`.
The review used the original passages, context excerpts, current CNL grammar,
closed vocabulary, and ontology. It did not use model predictions from the

Treating a team or device as a subclass of a capability may confuse components
with kinds of capability, so those proposed positive formulas were not accepted

contains only the 18 reviewed rows and remains development data.

The [ATP worksheet](../../data/benchmarks/atp2-01-3/chapter1-2019-review-v2/review-ai.csv)

contains all 40, each with a row-specific reason. These passages contain
obligations, conditions, temporal or causal claims, complex definitions, or
terms absent from the closed vocabulary. The absence of a positive label is a
limitation of this packet and representation, not evidence that the source
contains no formalizable knowledge.

The [ATP preflight](atp2-01-3_ai_review_preflight.json) remains blocked: there
are zero positive targets beyond ontology restatements, and the only labels
are AI-reviewed. A balanced positive/abstention comparison would require a
new, prospectively selected and reviewed positive packet, with the vocabulary
and representation frozen before model predictions are inspected. Source-only
method results on these AI labels can be reported as an exploratory abstention
diagnostic, never as a confirmatory benchmark.
