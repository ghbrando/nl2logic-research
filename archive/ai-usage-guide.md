# AI Usage Guide — NL2Logic Research

Created: 2026-03-11

---

## Core Principle

AI is a thinking partner, not an oracle. Every idea
must be understood deeply enough to defend in front
of a conference room of reviewers. The paper has
your name on it.

---

## Use Opus When:

- Developing new theoretical ideas
- Working through formal proofs and logical arguments
- Stress testing architecture — ask it to argue
  against your approach
- Writing or reviewing paper sections requiring
  sustained logical consistency
- Anything where being wrong has high cost —
  a subtle logical error in a proof is expensive

## Use Sonnet 4.6 When:

- Writing and debugging code
- Generating training data pair candidates
- Literature search and summarization
- Drafting boilerplate paper sections
- Quick technical questions with clear answers
- Iterating fast on well-understood problems

## Mental Model

High stakes reasoning    → Opus
High velocity execution  → Sonnet 4.6

---

## Conversation Archive Protocol

After every significant research conversation:

1. Export and save to archive/conversations/YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md
2. Write one paragraph summary in your own words
   without looking at the conversation
3. Commit to repo with date and topic in filename

## Trust Rules

- Never trust AI on highly specific technical claims
  without independent verification
- Always verify formal claims independently
- AI generates candidates — you validate them
- Your name is on the paper, not the AI's
