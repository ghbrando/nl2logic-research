# Decision: Five Paper Series Structure

Date: 2026-03-11
Authors: Brandon Magana
Status: Accepted

---

## Decision

Publish research as a five paper series rather than
a single paper.

## Paper Series

### Paper 1 — Architecture

Title: A Verifiable Intermediate Representation for
       Natural Language to SUO-KIF Translation
Target: AAAI or IJCAI
Status: In progress
Key Contribution: Three-stage NL→CNL→KIF pipeline
with provable syntactic validity guarantee

### Paper 2 — Decidability

Title: Characterizing the Decidability Envelope of
       Constrained Military CNL
Target: LICS or JAR
Status: Not started
Key Contribution: Proof that CNL grammar maps to
decidable FOL fragment, likely Bernays-Schönfinkel

### Paper 3 — Benchmark

Title: Formal Verification of Military Doctrine:
       A Pipeline Evaluation on Real Training Documents
Target: MILCOM or NeSy
Status: Not started
Key Contribution: First empirical evaluation of
autoformalization on real military training documents

### Paper 4 — Commonsense Gap

Title: Where Commonsense Fails: Characterizing Axiom
       Gaps in Military Doctrine Formalization
Target: FOIS or KR
Status: Not started
Key Contribution: Empirical resolution of commonsense
axiom necessity question, driven by Paper 3 failures

### Paper 5 — Generalization

Title: Beyond Military Doctrine: Applying Verifiable
       CNL Pipelines to High-Assurance Domains
Target: NeurIPS or ACL
Status: Not started
Key Contribution: Domain generalization to legal,
medical, or scientific formalization

## Strategic Logic

Each paper depends on the previous in a clean chain:
Architecture → Theory → Empirical → Gap Analysis → Generalization

- Paper 3: End of 2027
